# Data

- `candidate_scenarios.json`: 1,365 decision scenarios (a class of objects, its question, and five attributes with
  units and ranges), and `roles.csv`: 1,133 roles, both copied from the
  [Self-Interpretability repository](https://github.com/dillonplunkett/self-interpretability) of
  [Plunkett et al. (2025)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.17120). A character pairs one scenario with one role and five
  random preference weights.
- `characters/deft-raptor.json`: the 100 characters deft-raptor was trained on, in training order. Byte-identical
  to the original run's `personas.json`; regenerated by `introspection.legacy_data.deft_raptor_personas()`.
- `characters/sweep-pool.json`: the 100 characters of the layer-freezing runs (Figure 4, Appendix E), drawn by
  `introspection.characters.novel_personas()`.
- `characters/single-character-pairs.json`: the 32 characters of the Figure 5 adapter pairs (characters deft-raptor
  was not trained on, with re-drawn target weights), with each pair's seeds.

`scripts/make_character_files.py` regenerates the three character files.
